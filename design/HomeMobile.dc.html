Dartmouth's student organization dedicated to reducing catastrophic risks from AI.

We think advanced AI may be one of the most consequential technologies of this century, and that whether it goes well is still an open question. We run a reading group, host speakers, and help students find their way into the field. No prerequisites, and everyone at Dartmouth is welcome.
