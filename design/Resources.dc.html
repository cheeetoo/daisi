Resources

A short list, in the order we'd read it. All of it is free.

Start here

An afternoon's reading
The OpenAI/Hugging Face attack, clearly explainedDwarkesh PatelWhat happened when agents inside OpenAI's training runs coordinated to game an eval and cover their tracks. Read this first. Preventing an AI-related catastrophe80,000 HoursA long, careful overview of the problem, the counterarguments, and what people are doing about it. AI 2040: Plan AAI Futures ProjectA concrete scenario from the AI 2027 team for how the next fifteen years could go well: what would have to be agreed, verified and slowed.

Go deeper

A course, a book, the classic paper, and where the discussion happens
AI Safety FundamentalsBlueDotFree online course, and the closest thing to a standard introduction. Our reading group adapts it. The Alignment ProblemBrian ChristianThe best book-length introduction, and the one to hand to a friend who isn't sure any of this is real. Concrete Problems in AI SafetyAmodei et al., 2016The paper that set out the technical agenda, and still the cleanest statement of it. Alignment ForumLessWrongWhere much of the research discussion happens. Start with the curated posts, not the front page. LessWrongLessWrongThe wider forum the Alignment Forum grew out of: rationality, forecasting, and much of the field's history.

SPARKairosPart-time, remote research with a mentor over a semester. Fits alongside classes, and the natural next step after the reading group. ARENAARENAHands-on alignment engineering: transformers, interpretability, RL. The materials are free online; the in-person bootcamp is in London. AI Safety CampAI Safety CampRemote, part-time, project-based. A low-barrier first research experience. MATSMATSFull-time research with a mentor at a lab or academic group. Competitive; the standard route into technical safety research once you have some experience.
