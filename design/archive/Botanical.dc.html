Why we exist

Draft copy · replace with your own

We think advanced AI may be one of the most consequential technologies of this century, and that whether it goes well is still an open question. DAISI exists so that Dartmouth students who want to work on that question can find each other, learn the field properly, and get started.

We were founded in [term, year] by [a small group of students]. We try to be honest about what is and isn't known, open to people from any major, and useful rather than just interesting.

The [Winter 2027] reading group

A weekly discussion group working through [an adapted BlueDot AI Safety Fundamentals curriculum]: what today's systems can do, why alignment is hard, and what people are doing about it.

It is open to anyone at Dartmouth. Fill in the expression of interest form and we'll be in touch before the term starts.
